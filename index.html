Pomodoro

The technique uses a timer to break down work into intervals.

List tasks, focus for 25 minutes, breathe for 5… repeat until you win the day!

  • Start a 25-minute timer and focus solely on one task.
  • Take a 5-minute break when the alarm rings to recharge.
  • Repeat the cycle until your work is complete.
